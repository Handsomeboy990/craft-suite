# Example: a ninety-second demo script for a volunteer scheduling app

A small association asks for a 90 second demo of the scheduling app two of its
volunteers built. The rights record is filled and the two authors are the
owners.

## The first draft, and why it was rejected

The draft opened with "Planify is a modern, scalable, AI-powered platform that
revolutionises volunteer management", listed eleven features, and ran to 380
words. At the measured rate of 150 words a minute, that is two and a half
minutes of narration for a 90 second slot, about features the picture never
showed. "AI-powered" had no source: the app sorts shifts by availability, with
no model involved. The owners asked for it to be removed.

## The takeaway

"A coordinator fills next week's shifts in two minutes instead of an evening of
messages." The owners confirmed the "evening of messages" from their own
association's practice; the two minutes is shown live, not claimed.

## The storyboard, as delivered

| # | Picture | Words | On screen | Duration (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | title card over the empty week view | Every Sunday, our coordinator spent an evening chasing volunteers by message. | Planify | 9 |
| 2 | capture: the week view with open shifts in amber | This is next week. Eight shifts are still open. | 8 open shifts | 7 |
| 3 | capture: clicking "Suggest", the list ranks volunteers by availability | She clicks Suggest. Planify lists who is free, starting with those who have done fewest shifts this month. | | 13 |
| 4 | capture: assigning three volunteers, the shifts turn green | She assigns three people in a few clicks, and each one gets a message to confirm. | | 11 |
| 5 | capture, sped up and labelled: the rest of the week filled | The rest of the week takes the same steps. | sped up 4x | 14 |
| 6 | capture: a volunteer's phone confirming | On the volunteer's side, one tap confirms. | | 8 |
| 7 | capture: the full week in green, a clock on screen | Next week is covered, in under two minutes. | 1 min 52 s, real time | 12 |
| 8 | end card with the two authors' names and the licence | Planify was built by two of our volunteers. It is free and open source. | names, licence, link | 14 |

Narration: 92 words, about 37 seconds of speech at 150 words a minute. The rest
of each beat is the picture carrying the action, written into the beat as a
pause: the volunteers being assigned, the sped-up week, the confirmation on the
phone. The beats add up to 88 seconds, within the 60 to 180 second demo target
and the association's 90 second slot. The clock in beat 7 shows the real time
of the session, which is why the sped-up beat is labelled.
