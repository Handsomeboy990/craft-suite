# video-scripting

Turns a project and a brief into the script and storyboard a video is built
from, for a demo, a presentation, a short or a tutorial.

- Inputs: the brief and the rights record from `video-core`, the project, the
  owner's confirmations.
- Outputs: the script, the storyboard, the timing sheet, the claim list, the
  pronunciation lexicon.
- Depends on: video-core.

The takeaway comes first, in one sentence the owner agrees with, and anything
that does not serve it is cut on paper rather than in the edit. Each format has
its structure. Narration is written for the ear and timed at a stated speaking
rate, so the word count fixes the length before anything is recorded, and it
names what happens on screen. Every claim is tied to a source the owner
confirmed. The storyboard gives each beat its picture, words, on-screen text
and duration, and the timing sheet and the lexicon are what the capture,
motion, voice and edit skills read.
