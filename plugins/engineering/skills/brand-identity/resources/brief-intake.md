# Brief intake

What the identity needs from the brief, what may proceed on a recorded
assumption, and what may not. The questions go to the client once, grouped,
through `clarification-gate`, never one at a time as they come up.

## 1. The three blocking facts

Nothing visual is decided until each has an answer from the client or the
brief, quoted.

```
POSITIONING
  who is it for                       the audience in their own terms
  what does it promise them           the one thing, not a list
  against what                        the alternative they would otherwise
                                      choose, named by the client
  why believe it                      the reason to believe, a fact the
                                      client can stand behind

AUDIENCE
  who decides, who uses               often not the same person
  age range and context of use        on a phone in a shop, at a desk, in print
  languages and scripts               every one, because the type must cover it
  any access need stated              low vision, older readers, a sector rule

CONSTRAINTS
  existing assets to keep             a name, a mark, a colour, a typeface,
                                      with the reason they stay
  regulated sector                    health, finance, food, children: rules
                                      on claims and imagery
  surfaces                            screen only, or print, signage, vehicle,
                                      packaging, embroidery
  licence budget                      fonts and photography cost money, or
                                      the choice is limited to free licences
  accessibility target                AA by default; AAA or a sector standard
                                      if the brief says so
  deadline and sign-off               who accepts the brand on the client side
```

## 2. What may proceed on a recorded assumption

Recorded in the charter's open questions, visible, and marked not blocking:

```
the exact secondary accent           a range is proposed, the client chooses
the number of logo variants          start with primary, mono and reversed
illustration                         assumed none until the brief asks for it
print specifics                      CMYK and Pantone references deferred
                                     until a print surface is confirmed
```

## 3. What may never proceed on an assumption

```
a positioning sentence the client did not confirm
any fact about the client's history, people, customers or results
any competitor, or any claim about one
any market figure
any licence, for a font, an image, an icon or a reference
```

Each becomes a question, or a placeholder written as a placeholder in the
charter, for example `[client to confirm: founding year]`, never a plausible
value.

## 4. The question batch, as sent

```
To confirm before the identity work starts. Each question says why it is
asked. Answers in your own words are better than a choice from a list.

1. Who is this for? Describe the person who should feel it was made for them.
   (Decides register, imagery and legibility.)
2. What do you promise them that the alternatives do not? One thing.
   (Everything visual follows from it.)
3. What would they choose if you did not exist? Name it if you can.
   (Tells the identity what to differ from. We do not research competitors
   unless you ask.)
4. What already exists that must stay: a name, a logo, a colour, a font?
5. Where will it appear besides the website: print, signage, packaging,
   clothing, vehicles?
6. Which languages, and which alphabets, will it be read in?
7. Is there a budget for licensed fonts and photography, or should we stay
   with free licences?
8. Who on your side accepts the brand when it is ready?
```

## 5. Intake record

```
BRIEF INTAKE   <project>, <date>

Source            <the brief, its version or date>
Positioning       answered | missing     quote:
Audience          answered | missing     quote:
Constraints       answered | missing     quote:
Assumptions       <each, marked not blocking>
Questions sent    <date>, <how>
Answers received  <date>, quoted in full in the charter appendix
Verdict           proceed | wait
```
