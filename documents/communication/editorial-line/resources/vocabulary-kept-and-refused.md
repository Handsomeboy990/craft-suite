# Vocabulary kept and refused

The working list writers apply. One list per output language, built from
material in that language: the client's own words, their customers' words as
the client reports them, and the brand identity's voice brief when one exists.
A list is never translated word for word into another language.

## 1. Categories of refused words

Every list covers these, then adds the brand's own.

| Category | Why it is refused | Examples, English |
|---|---|---|
| The author's experience | `document-core` section 6: a reader for whom it is not easy concludes the failure is theirs | easily, simply, just, obviously |
| Empty intensifiers | they replace a fact the writer did not have | very, extremely, incredibly, seamlessly, robust, powerful |
| Unprovable superlatives | a claim, section 8 of the skill | best, leading, number one, unbeatable |
| Category cliches | they make the brand sound like its category, not like itself | passion, solutions, journey, world-class |
| The system's words | the reader does not share them | the internal names of services, job codes, abbreviations |
| Words the identity refuses | a signed identity's list is binding | quoted from the charter, with its version |

## 2. The list, as delivered

```
LANGUAGE   <output language>
Source     <brief, answers, charter version and state>

KEPT
  word or phrase        reason                                 source
  ...

REFUSED
  word or phrase        reason                 instead         source
  ...

REWRITES
  refused word          before                    after
  ...
```

Rules:

- Every kept and refused entry has a reason and a source. A word refused on
  taste alone is removed from the list.
- Every refused entry has the word used instead, or "rewrite the sentence"
  when no single word replaces it.
- At least one rewrite per refused entry, in the output language, written
  from a sentence the brand could really publish.
- A rewrite never introduces a fact. When the before sentence carries a claim
  that cannot be proved, the after sentence drops the claim; it does not
  replace it with a figure.

## 3. Rewrite examples

Generic examples in English, to show the shape. A real list is built from the
client's own sentences.

| Refused | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| simply | Simply book online. | Book online: choose a day, then a time. | the steps replace the reassurance |
| best | The best service in town. | We tell you what we did and why, before you pay. | the claim becomes the practice the client can show |
| seamless | A seamless experience from start to finish. | You get one text when the work starts and one when it is done. | the adjective becomes what the reader sees |
| passion | Our passion for quality. | Every repair is checked by a second mechanic. [TO CONFIRM: is it, held by the client] | the cliche becomes a practice, and the practice is marked until confirmed |
| unbeatable prices | Unbeatable prices. | The price is agreed before the work starts. | a comparison becomes a promise the client keeps |

The fourth row shows the rule of section 2: a rewrite that needs a fact the
writer does not have carries the marker into the draft.

## 4. Mechanics, per language

```
numbers        figures from what value, separators
dates          the format, from documents.date_format or the language's
               convention
prices         currency symbol position, tax mention if the sector requires
               it, supplied by the client
the brand name capitals, articles, possessive, never abbreviated unless the
               client does
address        formal or familiar, first person plural or the brand's name
```
