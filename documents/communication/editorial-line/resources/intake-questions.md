# Intake questions

What the editorial line needs from the brief, what may proceed on a recorded
assumption, and what may not. The questions go to the client once, grouped,
through `clarification-gate`, never one at a time as they come up.

When a `brand-identity` intake already happened, its answers are reused and
quoted, not asked again. Only what the identity did not cover goes out.

## 1. The three blocking facts

Nothing is written until each has an answer from the client or the brief,
quoted.

```
AUDIENCE
  who reads                          in the client's own words
  what they already know             what can be assumed, what never can
  what they come for                 the question they arrive with
  what they misunderstand today      the confusion the client meets
  where they read                    phone, desk, print, inbox, a counter
  languages                          every one, each gets its own vocabulary

OFFER
  what the client actually does      the services or products, as stated
  the one thing promised             not a list
  the conditions attached            cut-off times, exclusions, stock,
                                     anything the promise depends on

CONSTRAINTS
  regulated sector                   health, finance, food, children, law:
                                     rules on claims, supplied by the client
  subjects refused                   what the brand never speaks about
  claims already made in writing     a guarantee, a certification, a price
                                     promise, with the document that holds it
  who approves                       the person who says yes for the brand
  capacity                           how often the client can really publish,
                                     and who writes today
```

## 2. What may proceed on a recorded assumption

Recorded in the line's open questions, visible, marked not blocking:

```
the length range of an article       proposed, the client adjusts
the newsletter structure             proposed from the pillars
the order of pillars                 proposed from the reader's needs
mechanics                            date and number formats from the
                                     output language's convention, until
                                     the client states otherwise
```

## 3. What may never proceed on an assumption

```
any fact about the client: history, people, customers, results
any statistic, percentage or count
any testimonial, quotation or review
any competitor, or any claim about one
any audience figure: size, age distribution, reach, open rate
any guarantee or condition of the offer the client did not state
any rule on claims in a regulated sector
```

Each becomes a question, or a marker in the line written as a marker, for
example `[TO CONFIRM: the guarantee period on a repair, held by the client]`,
never a plausible value.

## 4. The question batch, as sent

```
To confirm before the editorial line is written. Each question says why it
is asked. Answers in your own words are better than a choice from a list.

1. Who reads what you publish? Describe them, and what they already know
   about what you do. (Decides register and what must be explained.)
2. What do they most often misunderstand about you or your work today?
   (Tells the line what to explain every time.)
3. What do you promise them, and on what conditions? (A promise is written
   with its conditions or not at all.)
4. Is there anything you never want to talk about, publicly?
5. Have you already promised anything in writing: a guarantee, a price, a
   time? Please send the document. (We quote it, we never paraphrase it.)
6. Which languages do you publish in?
7. How often can you really publish, and who writes today? (We only plan
   what you can sustain.)
8. Who on your side approves a text before it goes out?

We do not research competitors, write testimonials or quote figures unless
you supply them with their source.
```

## 5. Intake record

```
EDITORIAL INTAKE   <project>, <date>

Source            <the brief, its version or date>
Identity          signed | drafted | none   <charter version, if any>
Audience          answered | missing     quote:
Offer             answered | missing     quote:
Constraints       answered | missing     quote:
Assumptions       <each, marked not blocking>
Questions sent    <date>, <how>
Answers received  <date>, quoted in full in the line's appendix
Verdict           proceed | wait
```
