# Post brief and caption checklist

Every post starts as a brief. The caption is written last, and checked here
before it goes to the reviewer named in the editorial line's review rule.

## 1. Post brief

```
POST   <calendar id>, <channel>, <date>

Line version      <editorial line version and state>
Identity version  <charter version and state>
Pillar            <name, from the line>
Reader need       <from the line's audience, quoted>
Format            <from the format sheet, with its checked date>
The one thing     <what the reader knows or does afterwards>
Sources           <each fact in the caption: the document and its holder>
Consents          <each person named or shown: recorded where, by whom>
Visual            <what is shown; canvas | owner link | files; who makes it>
Caption           <the draft>
Alt text          <for each image>
Reply plan        <the comments it will likely bring, who answers, per the
                  moderation policy>
Drafter           <role>
Reviewer          <role, never the drafter>
Approver          <role, from the line's review rule>
State             brief | drafted | in review | approved | held (why)
```

## 2. Caption checklist

Each line is answered yes, or the caption goes back.

```
Line
[ ] serves one pillar of the line, named in the brief
[ ] uses only the line's register for this format
[ ] contains no word from the line's refused list
[ ] breaks none of the line's never-said lists: claims, comparisons,
    sensitive topics, promises without their conditions
[ ] needs no rule the line does not hold; if it does, the question went to
    editorial-line and the caption waits

Evidence
[ ] every fact has a source in the brief: a price, a date, an hour, a
    condition, a name
[ ] no figure about the audience, the reach or a result
[ ] no testimonial, review or quotation not supplied with its holder and
    consent
[ ] every gap is a visible marker, not a plausible sentence

People
[ ] every person named or shown has a recorded consent
[ ] no customer's data: their bike, receipt, address, face, without consent

Social specifics
[ ] hashtags and mentions are ones the client uses or approved
[ ] a mention of another account passes the line's comparisons rule
[ ] every link resolves, checked on <date>, and goes to the client's page
    where one exists
[ ] a trend, a meme or a sound fits the register and its licence was read
    at the source
[ ] a competition or a sponsored post is flagged for the client's adviser

Format
[ ] fits the format sheet, or the handover says what to check on the day
[ ] alt text written; video captioned; text in an image repeated in the
    caption

State
[ ] marked provisional or held if it touches an open point of the line or
    the identity
```

## 3. Refusal

A caption refused at review names the item of the checklist it failed and,
for a line rule, quotes the rule. It goes back to the drafter. The drafter
does not argue the rule in the post; a disagreement with the line goes to
`editorial-line` as a question.
